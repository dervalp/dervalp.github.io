## The question, in plain words

The About page used to close with a free one-hour call with no commitment, which the new wording drops. Should that free hour come back?

## The decision, in plain words

The About page now closes with Discuss your challenge, which opens the same contact form, plus a link to download the CV. The free-hour wording was removed.

## The intro, for fun

A free hour sounds generous until a CTO books twelve of them.

## The punchline, for fun

For now the page just says let's talk, and the clock stays out of it.

## The options, in plain words

A. Discuss your challenge, with no free-hour promise (what was built).
B. Bring back the free one-hour call with no commitment.

## What I had to decide

Keep the new closing, or restore the free one-hour offer as a deliberate commercial promise.

## What I did meanwhile

Visitors reach the same contact form from About; only the wording of the invitation changed.

## What it costs to change later

Restoring the old wording is a copy change on one page.

## What I could not know

(author) The PRD, the registers and the glossary do not settle this:

- (author) Whether the free hour is still a real offer was not confirmed; the spec only forbids fixed prices, deadlines and availability claims.
